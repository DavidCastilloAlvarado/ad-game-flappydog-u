Contact

Have feedback, found a bug, or just want to share your high score? I am happy to hear from you. Flappy Dog is an independent project, and every message helps shape what comes next.

Email

The best way to reach me is by email. You can send a message to:

flappydog.comms.inbox@pm.me

What to Expect

I read every message that comes through. While I cannot guarantee a same-day response, I aim to reply within a few days. If your message includes a bug report, please include details about your device, browser, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Screenshots or screen recordings are always appreciated.

What Kinds of Messages Are Welcome?

  • Bug reports — crashes, visual glitches, controls not responding
  • Feature ideas — new game modes, characters, themes, or mechanics you would like to see
  • Feedback — what you love, what could be improved, general thoughts
  • Collaboration — if you are a developer, artist, or writer and want to contribute
  • Just saying hello — always welcome

Where Is Flappy Dog Hosted?

Flappy Dog is deployed on Google Cloud Run and served through the domain flappydog.peruf.me. If you are curious about how the game is built or how it is deployed, feel free to ask and I will point you in the right direction.

Thanks for taking the time to reach out. Your feedback matters.
