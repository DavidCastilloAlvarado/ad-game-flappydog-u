Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flappy Dog (flappydog.peruf.me) handles your data. Flappy Dog is operated by the Flappy Dog team. By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Flappy Dog is designed to minimize data collection. When you play the game, the only information stored on your device is your high score, which is saved in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number. No account creation or sign-up is required to play the game.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google AdSense may place cookies on your device to deliver ads relevant to you and to report on ad performance. These cookies may include:

  • Advertising cookies that track your browsing habits across websites
  • Analytics cookies that help measure how ads are performing
  • First-party and third-party cookies set by Google and its advertising partners

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites on the internet. You can learn more about how Google handles user data by visiting Google's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information

The high score stored in your browser is used solely to display your personal best on the game-over screen. It is not used for analytics, marketing, or any other purpose. No other information is collected or used by the site operator.

Third-Party Services

Google AdSense is the only third-party service integrated into Flappy Dog. Google may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads and measure ad performance. We do not control the cookies that Google or its partners set, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policies for more information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — You can request information about what personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion — You can request that we delete any personal data we hold. Since Flappy Dog does not collect personal data on our servers, this primarily applies to any data held by third-party services.
  • Right to opt-out of personalized advertising — You can opt out of personalized ads from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
  • Right to clear local data — Your high score is stored locally in your browser. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or site data for flappydog.peruf.me.

Children's Privacy

Flappy Dog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has interacted with this site in a way that shares personal data, please contact us at david.castillo.comms.inbox@pm.me.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through the website or by email if we have collected your contact information.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at david.castillo.comms.inbox@pm.me.
